# PANDA DETECTIVE - CHARACTER BIBLE

## Main Character: Panda
Role: Lead Detective
Personality: Intelligent, brave, calm
Voice: Deep male human voice
Appearance: Stylized realistic 3D panda
Outfit: Black detective coat
Fur: Detailed black and white fur
Eyes: Expressive dark eyes

## Character Rules
- Keep the same face in every episode.
- Keep the same body proportions.
- Keep the same outfit.
- Keep the same voice identity.
- Use cinematic realistic 3D animation.
- Use natural facial expressions and body movement.
